FINAL SCOPE REVIEW: CAP, TRANSFER, LOCALIZATION, AND X0 GUARD
===========================================================

Conclusion
----------
The delivered scope is accurately described as an explicit finite marked
m=2,n=1 assembly, with exact finite cap/bridge/local-transfer certificates,
geometric source identification under a stated PL theorem trust basis, and
separate source-linked fundamental-group and homology certificates. It is
not a fully enumerated global source conjugacy and is not a PL standardization
of the closed X2. No closed X0 model is present in this new-source package.

This final review reads existing files only. It adds this text and its JSON
receipt; it does not modify the pinned cap manifest, any existing certificate,
the root README, or the root milestone. All 97 byte hashes pinned by the
geometric admission matched the current files during this audit. No native
or large homology call was made.

1. Exact finite data actually delivered
--------------------------------------

SPHERICAL BOUNDARY AND CAPS. data/shared_boundary.json.gz lists the complete
S2 x S1 boundary triangulation: 432 vertices and 1,792 tetrahedra, together
with its 256-tetrahedron coarse source boundary. data/cap_m0.json.gz and
data/cap_m2.json.gz each list 5,824 pentachora, complete gluing/deck data,
orientations, marked fixed disks, and the twin sphere. Their shared outer
boundary uses the same literal vertex prefix and facets. Every one of the
1,792 boundary tetrahedra has an exact barycentric inverse chart into a
coarse source tetrahedron. The charts determine maps on entire cells,
including the hemisphere interiors.

The actual paths retain displacement [0,4] for beta0 and [-8,4] for beta2
in eight-angle/four-time units. For beta2 this is one negative upstairs
turn and two negative downstairs turns. The complete spherical map is
bound to the angular map; endpoint parity does not replace the path.

SOURCE-BOUND BRIDGE. data/source_cap_bridge.json.gz contains 24,224
pentachora, joining the actual 96-tetrahedron source product port to the
1,792-tetrahedron cap port. Its 8,752-tetrahedron common refinement has
complete rational maps to both boundaries. The exact port maps, pulling
prism collars, volume partitions, orientation and deck are recorded and
checked. The positive source equator is [56,150,58,148], with fixed poles
[204,228]. The bridge preserves those angle/time conventions.

LOCAL F2/PHI. data/finite_F2_Phi_transfer.json.gz uses the actual spherical
beta2 family R_s. Its cut family Gamma has 442 vertices and 1,792 tetrahedra.
The file gives exact affine matrices and inverses for R_s and F2, followed
by 1,306 vertices, 3,584 four-dimensional convex cells, and 12,544 facets
for Phi. It records source and target vertex coordinates, halfspaces,
facets and incidences, affine maps and inverses, deck maps, and restrictions
to the four parameter-square sides. The definitions are

    F2(x,q)=(R_(1-q)(x),q),
    Phi(x,q,t)=(R_min(1-q,t)(x),q,t).

All 1,792 radial identities, branch-seam identities, and final-time F2
restrictions are checked. R0=R1=id; the poles are fixed; the disk radius
|x|+|y| is preserved; and the family commutes with deck. R_s is not asserted
to be uniform rotation at each radius or to constitute a circle group action.

The Phi output is a finite rational POLYTOPAL map, not an expanded list of
four-simplices. Canonical face-barycenter chains specify its equivariant
simplicial realization. This is a constructive finite refinement recipe;
the larger simplex lists have not been materialized. The full equatorial
lift is retained: the Gamma expression theta+q-1 differs from the accepted
F2 lift theta+q by the recorded integer +1, and

    1+(theta+q-1)=theta+q.

This local certificate does not enumerate a normal chart on the entire
source neighborhood or a global torus-to-puncture conjugacy.

MIXED SOURCE PROFILE. central_roll/mixed_profile_transfer.json records six
simultaneous u/v affine maps and inverses for the exact profile change
Psi_t(u,v,r)=(u+J(r,t),v+J(r,t),r). The six triangles give a joint PL map,
not a bilinear interpolation asserted to be PL. It fixes the actual puncture
and moves both source shears to the central collar. The separately saved
review verifies the stored matrices, their seams, and the actual enlarged
N-plus incidences: 1,580 tetrahedra after filling the puncture, 24 outer-grid
boundary triangles, and all eight puncture faces strictly interior.

WHOLE ASSEMBLY. The immutable marked_control.json.gz has 103,744 pentachora:
25,216 in the N product, 37,344 in the E product, 11,136 in the central roll,
24,224 in the bridge, and 5,824 in the cap. The orbit quotient has 51,872.
The independent assembly audit regenerates every part, all face pairings,
the covering squares, orientation anchor, full fixed subcomplex, and ten
actual source-roll seam carriers. Its actual ports satisfy the gluing
hypotheses of the geometric branch review. The geometric admission binds
these existing finite checks and current native exports.

2. Existence theorems and other explicit trust dependencies
----------------------------------------------------------

GLOBAL FRAMED NORMAL CHART. The corrected localization proof first enlarges
N by the saved N-adaptor and applies the mixed source-profile correction.
This order is essential: the actual puncture equator touches the ORIGINAL
cut torus, and its harmonic longitude is not constant there. A chart
simultaneously prescribing that original torus and a straight normal join
ball would be incompatible. In N-plus the puncture and outer grid are
disjoint. Relative PL normal-neighborhood existence/uniqueness, triviality
of the oriented normal disk bundle over a circle, extension of the marked
meridian-preserving outer torus chart, and lifting through the marked double
branched cover then supply the framed pair chart.

This is a legitimate geometric PL existence argument with verified local
hypotheses. It is not an exported finite atlas of the source cells. The
global chart, its full inverse matrices, and an expanded source-global
conjugating map are not present. The localization proof states this boundary
explicitly; the final report should retain it.

DISK-LOOP COMPARISON. The proof's space-and-time Alexander contraction is
joint PL: for delta(z)=Lz+b on a cell, centered scaling yields
Lz+(I-L)c+a(Lc+b-c), affine jointly in z,a. Its domain cells are cones over
the original affine cells, with the inverse constructed in the same way.
Pullback by a=1-q gives the collar correction matching the actual beta2
loop. This is an explicit PL construction/proof, but its entire additional
cell list is not exported. It must not be confused with the separately
enumerated finite F2/Phi file.

NATIVE SOURCE TRANSPORT. The signed diagram/exterior construction and the
intermediate native PL moves retain their pinned native semantics. The
source/import orientation signs and exact marked peripheral endpoint are
independently checked, but a complete portable elementary move transcript
for every intermediate peripheral triangulation is not exported.

NATIVE MANIFOLD ADMISSION. The current cover and quotient exports pass
the recorded Regina validity, closedness, nonideal, connectedness and
orientability checks. Those native predicates, and their recognition of
the required vertex links, are part of the explicit trust basis. They are
not four-sphere recognition or a closed-X2 standardization certificate.

ALL-STRATA BRANCH GEOMETRY. The normal model at every stratum follows from
the actual source normal pair, products with time, deck-paired relative
ball replacements, the spherical refinement collars, and the cap cone pair.
In particular the cap cone vertex uses the standard fixed core unknot in
boundary(D2 x D2); coning gives a flat fixed disk. This geometric argument
is joined to the actual checked assembly ports. The 208 fixed-triangle
normal-circle checks are additional finite evidence; alone they would not
settle local flatness at all fixed vertices.

3. Cap product type versus closed-X2 standardness
------------------------------------------------
The cap's PL S2 x D2 type is justified by its recorded product, refinement-
collar, and cone construction. Each half becomes the cone on the standard
boundary(D2 x D2) pair after the actual equivariant spherical untwisting;
the two halves are joined along their indicated solid torus. This is a
geometric product-type proof, not an inference from cap homology.

The native unmarked cap copies simplified to 14 and 13 pentachora and have
H1=0, H2=Z, chi=2, with one boundary component. Those calculations are
cross-checks only. No portable elementary-move standardization of either
cap to a chosen reference triangulation is claimed. Most importantly,
standard local cap pieces do not prove that the resulting closed X2 is
PL S4. The source-linked pi1=1 and integral homology (Z,0,0,0,Z) likewise
do not supply the requested PL standardization.

The saved native search's best checkpoint at this review is 2,072
pentachora. The parent's final native replay and milestone update are
separate ongoing reporting steps. This review performs no native replay
and admits no success to the one-pentachoron reference ball. A bounded
unsuccessful search implies no exoticness conclusion.

4. X0 absence and the operative guard
------------------------------------
The new-source package's structured-artifact inventory was scanned. The
only top-level twist-0 facet model is cap/data/cap_m0.json.gz. Direct face
incidence counting confirms that it has 5,824 pentachora and 1,792 boundary
tetrahedra: it is an OPEN cap template, not a closed X0. Its separate native
copy receipt also has one boundary component. No closed twist-0 facet model
or X0-named artifact was found in this project tree.

The current closed assembler has no m=0 construction route. It loads
cap_m2.json.gz, requires twist==2 and cap_full_turns==-1, and writes a
model with twist=2, roll=1. The independent whole-assembly verifier also
requires those parameters and the full m2 winding. The geometric admission
excludes X0 construction and states that it remains behind required X2
standardization; the milestone records closed_X0_constructed=false.

This is an actual restriction of the delivered entrypoint, in addition to
the workflow gate. It is not described as a generic future X0 implementation
that automatically unlocks when a Boolean changes. Such an implementation
is not delivered. Having beta0's open template available does not cross
the closed-X0 gate.

5. Reporting findings
----------------------
No overclaim of a fully finite global N-plus atlas or PL standardness of
the closed control was found in the current localization proof, geometric
admission, cap README, or root README. The scope statements distinguish
finite data, native semantics and theorem-level steps correctly.

The root README/milestone still describe native standardization as running
at this audit snapshot. The parent is stopping the declared batch and
performing final replay, so those transient status fields need the parent's
final update. This does not affect the immutable construction admission or
the source-linked group/homology certificates. The geometric admission's
own exclusion of group/homology is also correct: those conclusions are
admitted by separate later receipts, not retroactively by that earlier gate.

Recommended final wording: an explicitly assembled and verified marked
m=2,n=1 control, geometrically identified with the intended construction
under the stated PL theorem/native trust basis, with certified trivial
fundamental group and sphere homology; PL standardization remains open
unless the separate source-bound replay reaches the required reference
ball. Closed X0 remains unconstructed and guarded.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW: LOCAL BRANCH MODELS AND SOURCE LOCALIZATION
=============================================================

Reviewer scope
--------------
This review examines the geometric arguments and their finite premises. It
does not use raw four-dimensional homology or an unmarked recognition result
to infer the marked local branch model. The source-global normal chart is a
theorem-level construction, distinguished from the finite local collar maps.

1. Why the assembled four-dimensional branch models are standard
---------------------------------------------------------------
The required local involution model is

    (a,b,z) -> (a,b,-z),   (a,b) in R2, z in D2.

Its fixed set is R2 x {0}; the quotient has the standard double-branch model.
Checking only a fixed triangle's normal circle proves this model along that
triangle's interior. It does not by itself rule out a knotted link at a fixed
vertex. The following geometric provenance supplies the missing evidence at
all lower-dimensional strata.

SOURCE AND PRODUCTS. The three-dimensional source has the pair model
R x D2 with deck -1 on D2. The finite source audit checks every fixed edge's
free half-turn normal circle and every fixed vertex's S2 link with exactly
two fixed directions. The quotient link is a connected PL surface of Euler
characteristic two. Removing its two branch points gives the unique connected
double cover of an annulus with odd meridional monodromy; filling the two
points yields the standard half-turn S2 action. Coning this link gives the
standard local branched arc at each source vertex. Product with a time
interval gives the displayed four-dimensional pair model. The time seam is
an equivariant pair homeomorphism, so it preserves these neighborhoods.

RELATIVE COARSENING. The independently replayed three-dimensional moves
replace u*L by v*D, where L is a checked PL 2-sphere and D=L minus the open
star of v is a checked PL disk. Both sides are PL 3-balls with the identical
triangulated boundary L. The extension of the boundary identity gives a PL
homeomorphism relative to all of L. The two supports in a deck pair have
disjoint interiors; the checker verifies that no simplex contains both u
and deck(u), and verifies the exact deck-conjugate replacement. Choose the
homeomorphism on one ball and its deck conjugate on the other. They agree
on shared boundary points because both are identity there. Their interiors
contain no fixed point. Thus the whole move preserves the equivariant pair,
including its normal neighborhoods, not merely its fixed-face list. Boundary
ports and all fixed vertices are protected. Product with time preserves this
relative equivariant homeomorphism.

FREE TORUS PIECES. The torus adaptors and central roll piece have free deck
action. Their exact product/refinement charts introduce no new fixed points.
They affect the source near the puncture equator, which is also a free point
of the action, but do not change the branch-arc local model at either pole.

SPHERICAL BRIDGE. The saved bridge is an equivariant PL product
S2 x S1 x I, realized by exact common refinement and two refinement collars.
At each of the two fixed poles of S2, a neighborhood is D2 with the half-turn.
The fixed locus is therefore the two products {pole} x S1 x I, each a locally
flat annulus. The full lower-dimensional pair models follow from this product
identification. Its 56 fixed-triangle normal-circle checks are independent
finite consistency checks of that geometry.

CAP CONE VERTICES. For either cap, one hemisphere boundary block is a
D2 x S1 solid torus, with deck -1 on the disk, and the other block is
E x D2, where E=boundary D2 and deck acts freely on E. The actual spherical
beta_m map extends over the first block, fixes its axis circle, and commutes
with deck. Untwisting by that map and using the checked refinement collar
identifies their glued union, equivariantly, with

    boundary(D2 x D2) = (D2 x S1) union (S1 x D2).

The fixed circle is {0} x S1, the standard core unknot of the first solid
torus in this S3. Coning the equivariant boundary homeomorphism identifies
the cap half with the standard four-ball pair having a flat fixed disk.
This proves the local model at the cone vertex itself; the normal-circle
checks on two-simplices alone would not prove it. The two cap halves meet
along the free E x D2 block, so this gluing introduces no additional fixed
singularity. The finite cap verifies exactly two fixed disks and their
normal circles.

PORT GLUING. The source port and cap port of the bridge are complete
triangulated boundaries, with explicit inverse label maps and compatible
deck permutations. Gluing collared equivariant pairs by these boundary
homeomorphisms glues their standard half-neighborhoods into the standard
full pair neighborhood. The parent assembly must check that its actual
facets use these ports, that every expected boundary facet is used once,
that no extra identification occurs, and that the deck maps agree. Subject
to those assembly checks, the argument proves all local branch models.
It does not require a separate enumeration of all four-dimensional fixed
vertex links, and it does not infer them from homology.

2. Concrete chart issue found by independent review
--------------------------------------------------
The actual puncture equator [56,150,58,148] consists of cut midpoint vertices
at fixed-coordinate level 1/2. It therefore lies on the ORIGINAL torus
boundary of the clipped N. Its harmonic torus u coordinates are not constant:
56 and 58 have one rational u value, while 150 and 148 have another. Hence a
global pair chart cannot be both the specified original torus chart and the
prescribed normal join chart on this common circle.

The correction is to use N+ obtained by adding the already verified
N-adaptor collar. Its outer grid boundary is disjoint from the puncture.
Before applying normal-neighborhood extension, the simultaneous six-triangle
profile change in u and v moves the original roll/twist support to the
central collar. It is pointwise identity at original r=0, so it fixes the
actual puncture even though the equator meets that original torus. This
removes the chart-overlap obstruction without changing the source, cap, or
bridge geometry. The revised proof and mixed-profile certificate have now
been reviewed and bound in geometric_review.json. Independent saved-data
checking verifies all six mixed forward/inverse matrices, their common
edges, the boundary identities, and the unwrapped source seam. Filling the
four puncture tetrahedra back into the compact N stage gives 1,580 tetrahedra
with precisely the 24 outer-grid boundary triangles; all eight puncture
triangles are interior faces and share no vertex with that grid. The relative
coarsening fixes the puncture sphere, so extending it by identity across the
filled ball transports the enlarged regular-neighborhood pair to the actual
compact saved piece.

3. Finite disk-family and PL Alexander argument
----------------------------------------------
The actual beta2 family R_s preserves each hemisphere's diamond radius
|x|+|y|, poles, time, and deck. The transfer verifier checks the full affine
radius identity in all 1,792 tetrahedra, as well as R0=R1=id. Thus one
hemisphere supplies a joint PL disk-loop. It need not be uniform rotation
at each interior radius or define a circle group action. A chosen global
normal trivialization, rather than invariance under arbitrary changes of
normal frame, is the appropriate way to use this family on the source.

For comparison of two joint PL disk loops with the same equator motion,
their difference delta(x,t) is identity on the whole boundary of
B=D2_diamond x I, fixes the axis, preserves t, and commutes with deck. The
space-and-time Alexander contraction is correct:

    A_a(z)=c+a[delta(c+(z-c)/a)-c],   c=(0,0,1/2),

inside c+a(B-c), and identity outside. For delta(z)=Lz+b on one affine
cell, this becomes Lz+(I-L)c+a(Lc+b-c), affine jointly in (z,a).
The parameter-domain cells are cones over the original affine cells, and
the inverse is given by the identical formula with delta inverse. It fixes
the outer boundary and time endpoints, preserves time, and commutes with
the half-turn. This proves a genuine PL contraction; squeezing only the
disk radius while leaving an arbitrary time-dependent expression would
not provide the same affine justification.

Pulling this contraction back by a=1-q on a small added puncture collar
gives a joint PL correction that exactly matches the two boundary loops
and is identity at the bulk end. Its support is disjoint from the central
roll. Thus the later use of Phi inverse composed with the source isotopy
can be made an actual relative-puncture isotopy, not only an assertion that
two equatorial winding numbers agree.

4. Theorem trust boundary
-------------------------
The finite source inputs establish local flatness, the actual regular
neighborhood cut, the positively oriented normal puncture, and the marked
peripheral torus. They do not list a global normal disk atlas on N+.
Existence and relative uniqueness of a normal neighborhood, triviality of
an oriented normal disk bundle over a circle, and extension of the marked
meridian-preserving torus chart supply that global chart at theorem level.
Once the original overlapping boundaries are separated as above, these
are legitimate standard PL existence steps; an exported finite atlas is
not logically necessary for a geometric PL identification. It WOULD be
necessary for a claim that software had replayed the entire conjugating
homeomorphism simplex by simplex. Those are different certification claims.

The normal-neighborhood reference was independently opened and checked:
C. P. Rourke and B. J. Sanderson, Block Bundles I, Annals of Mathematics
87 (1968), section 4, Theorems 4.3-4.4 and relative Addendum 4.5.
https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/rs003.pdf

The solid-torus disk-and-ball extension explanation also appears in
A. Hatcher, Notes on Basic 3-Manifold Topology, section 2.1, page 25.
https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/3M/3M.pdf

Neither this review nor the localization theorem proves that the assembled
closed X2 is PL S4. Standardization of X2 is a separate required certificate.

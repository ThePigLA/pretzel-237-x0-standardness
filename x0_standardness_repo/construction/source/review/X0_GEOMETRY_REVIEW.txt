INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE m=0, n=1 CONSTRUCTION
=============================================

Decision
--------
The prepared X0 specialization changes the actual cap geometry, not merely
its twist label. The source-relative u-only roll geometry and the identity
spherical filling give the intended zero-twist construction. The unchanged
source geometry may be inherited from the hash-pinned accepted release;
the closed candidate's attachment, orientation, quotient, local manifold
admission and markings require their own current checks, which are present.

This review does not prove that X0 is PL S4. Its purpose is to establish that
the candidate being handed to the reviewer is the intended marked X0.
Global fundamental-group and homology certificates are separate work.

1. Actual finite change
----------------------
The assembler reads cap/data/cap_m0.json.gz from the immutable original
release. The independent assembly checker also reads that m0 file and
regenerates every product, bridge and cap pentachoron without importing the
assembler. Both require twist 0, roll 1 and the zero full cap turn.

The completed candidate has 103,744 pentachora and 4,310 vertices. Direct
set comparison against the exact X2 marked master establishes:

  unchanged noncap pentachora                 97,920
  common cap pentachora                        2,816
  total common pentachora                    100,736
  removed m2 cap pentachora                    3,008
  inserted m0 cap pentachora                   3,008

The entire difference lies in the cap. The four noncap lists are identical:
N has 25,216 cells, E 37,344, central roll 11,136, and the bridge 24,224.
Every shared simplex has the same ambient orientation sign. The vertex
labels, deck permutation, positive source orientation anchor, 96-tetrahedron
source port, 1,792-tetrahedron cap port, and both marked sphere lists are
unchanged. There is no independent relabelling that could conceal a changed
source or a changed meridian/longitude convention.

2. Zero twist is verified on entire spherical cells
--------------------------------------------------
The frozen cap replay checks the actual rational barycentric refinement,
the equatorial angular equation and its complete lifted time path. For m0
the displacement is [0,4] in the fixed angle/time mesh units: zero upstairs
full turns and zero downstairs full turns. This retains the integer lift;
it is not a conclusion drawn from even deck parity.

The additional independent check_zero_cap_geometry.py reads the shared
spherical mesh and the m0 inverse barycentric charts directly. It uses no
producer or previous verifier import. For all 1,792 boundary tetrahedra it
constructs the physical diamond-disk/time coordinates of the coarse source
and shared target, checks the hemisphere and time slab, and proves exact
source=target coordinates at the four affinely independent vertices.
Consequently every full affine tetrahedron map, including hemisphere
interiors and all lower-dimensional faces, is the identity. This justifies
R_t=id, F0=id and Phi0=id for the actual saved geometry.

The m0 and m2 meshes have identical boundary facets, vertex labels and deck,
but different internal cap facet sets and different inverse spherical
refinement charts. Thus choosing m0 changes the attachment represented by
the filling; it does not relabel the nontrivial m2 attachment as zero twist.

3. Which inherited source receipts suffice
------------------------------------------
The frozen release manifest is pinned by its original SHA256 and all 205
payload files are checked. The original geometric admission's 97 source
artifacts are also hash-bound. These unchanged facts remain applicable:

* the oriented P(-2,3,7) source and exact peripheral transport;
* the preferred diagram-positive normal meridian and longitude;
* the local branched source pair and the actual puncture labels;
* the adaptor's equivariant relative coarsening and protected ports;
* the central positive roll certificate and its finite u-only profile map;
* the source-bound spherical bridge, its exact collars and deck-compatible
  boundary identifications;
* the geometric product/refinement/cone interpretation of the open caps.

In particular, the central certificate already contains the u-only map

    (u,v,r,t) -> (u + J(r,t), v, r, t).

Its six affine pieces have checked inverses, matching faces, the exact
top/bottom seam equation and identity on the original r=0 boundary. It
therefore fixes the actual puncture even though the puncture equator meets
the original cut torus. The central straightening retains one full positive
longitudinal roll and no meridional shift: source winding (1,0). The new
assembly check verifies all ten recorded source seam carriers in the actual
candidate facets. No extrapolation from the five witness levels alone is
used: the prior certificate supplies the full affine maps and seam identities.

4. m2-specific steps that are unnecessary for m0
----------------------------------------------
There is no meridional twist to transfer from the source torus to the
puncture. The following m2 steps are therefore unnecessary here:

* the simultaneous u/v profile correction;
* the additional N-plus normal disk-bundle chart agreeing with both the
  outer torus and puncture charts;
* the nontrivial torus-to-puncture meridional disk loop;
* the joint space-and-time Alexander contraction used to compare that loop
  with the finite beta2 spherical family;
* the nonidentity F2/Phi collar transfer and its full-turn lift adjustment.

For m0, the needed source-relative map is already the u-only profile
transfer. Its spherical boundary restriction is the identity, and the
actual cap's spherical maps are independently verified to be the identity.
The former chart-overlap issue does not recur: no global disk chart
simultaneously prescribing the original overlapping torus and puncture
boundaries is required for this identification.

This removes the extra m2 localization theorem dependency, not all
mathematical interpretation. The signed-source native transport, elementary
PL product and collar geometry, equivariant gluing principles, and native
local-manifold admission retain their stated roles.

5. All-strata local branch geometry
-----------------------------------
The normal-circle checks at 208 fixed triangles are useful finite checks,
but they alone would not establish local flatness at fixed vertices. The
piece geometry supplies the complete argument:

SOURCE: the previously admitted 3D source has the standard branched arc
pair at every stratum. Product with time preserves the standard 4D fixed
surface pair. The equivariant relative 3-ball replacements preserve this
pair and the protected puncture; their paired supports have disjoint
interiors and no fixed interior points.

FREE PIECES: the torus adaptors and central roll have free deck action, so
their exact product/refinement charts add no new fixed strata.

BRIDGE: its exact source-bound S2 x S1 x I product has two locally flat
fixed annuli. The current assembly verifies both actual complete ports and
the commuting covering-face maps, so the product neighborhoods glue to
the intended source and cap neighborhoods.

CAP: the extra independent check proves the actual m0 cap consists of its
two recorded cone halves, each of 2,912 pentachora. Their intersection is
exactly the filling solid torus. The filling is the literal 96-tetrahedron
E x D2 staircase product together with its 1,920-tetrahedron refinement
collar. With the identity spherical attachment, each cone base is the
standard boundary(D2 x D2) pair, with fixed circle {0} x S1. Coning gives
the standard 4-ball pair with flat fixed disk, including the cone vertex.
This uses the known product geometry, not an inference from homology or
an appeal to four-dimensional Schoenflies.

The independent candidate assembly also checks the entire fixed subcomplex,
so omitted isolated fixed vertices or edges cannot hide outside the marked
triangles. It checks the twin sphere and its two intersections, all deck
orbits and orientation transport, and the exact orbit-cell quotient.

6. Audit of verify_candidate_geometry.py
----------------------------------------
The script binds the source-bound X2 acceptance receipt, immutable source
release and old geometric admission. It requires current candidate-native
admissions, the independently regenerated assembly, exact shared noncap
lists, ports and orientation, and a current actual cap replay. The added
zero-cap receipt is bound to its checker hash and all six frozen inputs;
the geometric output also binds the current cap replay by file hash.

These are sufficient dependencies for this m0 specialization. The old X2
geometric receipt alone would not have admitted a changed closed model;
the current m0 assembly and cap checks are essential and have been retained.
The reviewer-provided X2 link certificates are not used as X0 link
certificates: the new cover and quotient have their own native admissions.

No critical code or proof issue was found. The independent identity and
cone/product checks supplement the previously accepted cap semantics with
direct checks of precisely the geometry relevant to m0. No producer script
was modified by this reviewer.

Reproduction and scope
----------------------
From the X0 package root:

    python3 review/check_zero_cap_geometry.py \
        --candidate candidate/marked_candidate.json.gz \
        --output diagnostics/current_zero_cap_geometry.json

The complete package verifier must also run its current cap, native,
assembly, geometric and group gates. This supplementary check is not a
substitute for them. It makes no X0 standardization claim and starts no
simplification search.
